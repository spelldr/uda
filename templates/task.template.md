## Notes for Authors

- One action per step. Steps can have numbered sub-steps if needed, but avoid narrative.
- Keep steps prescriptive. If you're explaining *why* a step works, extract that to a Concept doc.
- If troubleshooting is needed, extract it to a Troubleshooting doc.
- `note` fields are for warnings, tips, and contextual pointers — not narrative.
- Assume successful execution; failures get their own Troubleshooting doc.
